---
title: 'Rate limits'
description: 'Per-plan quotas and how to handle them.'
---

The API enforces two kinds of limits on **live keys**:

- A **daily request cap** that resets at 00:00 UTC
- A **burst rate limit** measured per second

Test keys (`ik_test_*`) are not rate-limited and do not count toward either.

## Plan limits

| Plan  | Daily requests | Burst (per sec) | History window |
|-------|----------------|-----------------|----------------|
| Free  | 500            | 1               | 24 hours       |
| Pro   | 100,000        | 10              | 2 years        |

History window is the cutoff for `published_at` on news articles — Free
plan responses will not include events older than 24 hours.

## Monitoring usage

The [developer dashboard](https://newsapi.insighthread.com/dashboard) shows:

- A **daily usage bar** with your current count vs. the daily cap
- **Per-key request totals** for the current month
- **All-time** request count per key

The counters update within seconds of each request.

## Upgrading

If you're hitting the Free plan cap and need more, upgrade from the
dashboard. The new limits apply immediately on successful checkout.

[Compare plans →](https://newsapi.insighthread.com/dashboard/upgrade)
